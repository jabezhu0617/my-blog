TLDR: I want to go into AI safety to do meaningful work

For far too long, I have been running away from the reality that AI is very real and will be replacing most of the jobs we have right now. This was one of the main reasons I chose to go into mechanical engineering. I believed by dealing with the physical world, it would take longer for AI to replace me. However, after a year of school and working in a robotics research lab over the summer, I felt unsatisfied with the things I was learning and the work I was doing. I hated taking exams on topics I cared little about and I disliked how slow paced the hardware environment is and how long it takes to iterate as it takes time to manufacture and test a prototype. While I appreciated having a physical product I can feel with my hand, the satisfaction came from creation not from the end result. There was also a lack of impact from the work I was doing, as I only worked on a small part of the whole robot. Even if we looked at the robot as a whole, it served a very specific purpose that won’t impact the general public. Carrying these feelings of dissatisfaction, I went to SF to visit a friend. 

After talking with my friend, I discovered the field of AI safety as we caught up and later on met lots of other people in AI safety. The more I heard about it the more I felt deep down that this is the moment I have been waiting for: a pathway to spend my time doing meaningful work. Once I returned home, I pondered hard about this life pivot and after hours of journaling and thinking, I resolved myself to spend this next school year learning all that I can about the field in order to drop out and start doing meaningful work full time. 

I want this blog to be a place for me to share and keep track of what I have learned and the things that I am doing.
