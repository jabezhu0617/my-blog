TLDR: I want to go into AI safety to do meaningful work

For far too long, I have been running from the reality that AI is real and will replace most of the jobs we have right now. This was one of the main reasons I chose to go into mechanical engineering. I believed that by dealing with the physical world, it would take longer for AI to replace me. However, after a year of school and working in a robotics research lab over the summer, I felt unsatisfied with what I was learning and doing. I hated taking exams on topics I cared little about, and I disliked how slow-paced the hardware environment is and how long it takes to iterate as it takes time to manufacture and test a prototype. While I appreciated having a physical product I can feel with my hand, the satisfaction came from creation, not from the end result. Carrying these feelings of dissatisfaction, I went to SF to visit a friend. 

After talking with my friend, I discovered the field of AI safety as we caught up and later on met lots of other people in AI safety. The more I heard about it, the more I felt deep down that this is the moment I have been waiting for: a pathway to spend my time doing meaningful work. Once I returned home, I pondered hard about this life pivot, and after hours of journaling and thinking, I resolved to spend this next school year learning all that I can about the field in order to drop out and start doing meaningful work full time. 

I want this blog to be a place to share and track what I have learned and what I am doing.
